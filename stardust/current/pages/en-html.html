AMAG – your car dealership at 90 locations and online

Welcome to the biggest car dealership in Switzerland. As official Swiss dealership, we at AMAG offer you all the models of all the Volkswagen brands. At AMAG, you will find online and on site a huge range of new, stock and used vehicles from car brands like Volkswagen, Audi, SEAT, Škoda, CUPRA, VW Commercial Vehicles and Bentley. Of course, our experts also offer all the services related to the car after the purchase – be it a repair in the workshop or a consultation at the garage!

On our online marketplace, find your dream car from our house brands Volkswagen, Audi, SEAT, Škoda, CUPRA and VW Commercial Vehicles.

Would you like to sell your used car? Then you’re right to turn to AMAG. We make you an offer, and you can rely on us. We sell your car with the usual AMAG reliability.

Also with regard to service, you’re in good hands with us at the garages and online. Whether accident damage or car service: just arrange an appointment online or contact your AMAG garage directly. We can quickly and easily provide you with further assistance.

Wherever you are, an AMAG garage is never far away. With our 90 locations throughout Switzerland, we are not only the biggest car dealership in Switzerland but also the largest service network. Simply contact the garage of your choice – we will gladly advise you.
